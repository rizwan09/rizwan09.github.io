I work with motivated students and researchers on **AI agents, generative AI, NLP, machine learning, and AI for software engineering**, with a focus on agents whose claims, code, and actions can be audited. Current directions include:

- **Code and software-engineering agents:** repository-scale issue resolution, PR review, and verifiable human–AI co-development.
- **Attributed knowledge for agents:** retrieval, memory, and skills with provenance and safety guarantees.
- **Grounded agents:** web, GUI, map, and data-visualization agents, and living benchmarks for them.
- **Trustworthy agents:** red-teaming, agentic security, calibrated abstention, and failure attribution.

**How to reach out.** Email me at [rizwan@ucla.edu](mailto:rizwan@ucla.edu) with the subject line "Prospective student: [your name]", your CV, and a short note on which of my papers or directions interests you and why. I read every message, but cannot reply to all.

**Please note:** I do not have funding for paid internships in 2027. Unpaid research assistantships are possible, and I am always open to research collaborations. Ph.D. students are co-supervised with university partners.
